Partner questions

What partners ask us

Can I browse homes before I am verified?

No. Browsing and messaging homes unlocks once our team has verified your organization. Until then you can complete your profile and message the team.

What is a matching pledge?

A promise to match donor gifts to homes, up to a limit you propose. Our team reviews it. Once approved, it is shown on your profile.

Can we have our logo shown?

Yes. Upload your logo and a short "sponsored by" message. They are shown publicly after the team approves the wording.
