About CAM Orphanage Connect

We exist so that every registered children’s home in Cameroon can be found, trusted and supported.

Our mission

Connect care with the people who want to help.

Across Cameroon, dedicated people run children's homes with very little visibility. At the same time, donors at home and abroad, and organizations that want to do good, struggle to know who to trust.

CAM Orphanage Connect closes that gap. Homes are verified, needs are specific, progress is visible, and conversations are safe. That means a donor can say exactly what their gift is for, and a home can say exactly what it needs.

What we believe

Four promises

Dignity

Children and the people who care for them are treated with respect in every picture and every word.

Transparency

Needs have goals, goals have progress bars, and anyone can see how far a need has come.

Safety

Everyone is approved before they can browse or message, and the team looks after the conversations.

Local ownership

Homes tell their own stories in English or French and decide what they need.

Honest about where we are

The platform is growing. Today it records pledges but does not collect money, and password resets are handled by the team. We would rather tell you plainly than promise what is not ready. When online payments arrive, this page and the questions page will say so clearly.
