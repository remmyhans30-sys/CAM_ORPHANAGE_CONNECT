Getting started

Four small steps

  • Create your donor account with your name and email.
  • Add where you live and how you like to give (optional).
  • Our team approves your account. You can message the team in the meantime.
  • Browse verified homes and make your first pledge.
About pledges. A pledge is a recorded promise to give. No payment is taken on this website. You and the home agree how to send the gift, and the team can help.
Donor questions

What donors ask us

Which currency do I pledge in?

Pledges are recorded in Central African CFA francs (XAF), starting from 500 XAF. You can note a preferred payment method such as MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money in your profile.

Can I give more than once to the same need?

Yes. Each pledge adds to the need until its goal is reached.

Will the home know who I am?

Yes, unless you choose to hide your name when pledging. Homes can only start a conversation with donors who gave under their own name.

How long does approval take?

We review donor accounts as quickly as we can. Message the team from your Messages page if you are waiting.
